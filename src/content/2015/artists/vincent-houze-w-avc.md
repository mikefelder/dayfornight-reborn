Vincent Houzé is a digital artist combining an understanding of computer science, art, physics and music. This is brought into his work in various forms through the use of dedicated audio and motion analysis tools that control the behavior of custom dynamic systems. He has worked on architectural and interactive installations, performances, concerts, feature films, video games and commercials in Paris, London and New York. He is currently a media and interaction designer at AV&C, which he will join forces with.

AV&C is an award winning creative technology studio recognized for digital landmarks in the physical world. We design and create experiential technology and software systems for brands, architects, and artists, and seek to promote responsive activation of public and private spaces. At Day for Night we come together in collaboration and support of VincentHouzé, one of our team’s leading talents, in a purely artistic environment.
