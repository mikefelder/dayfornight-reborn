Children of Pop is dead. After the Chill Mega Chill release of “Fiesta/Drift” in the Winter of 2013, the band toured off and on + played a few opening slots at some amazing festivals + headlined some less than stellar parties that tend to go on too long. Children of Pop spent most of 2015 in the lab w/ the pen and the pad trying to get this damn label off + recording a new full length record.

But then, Children of Pop died a sad and lonely digital death.

So, the sophomore album, “What does 69 mean?”, will be posthumously released Spring of 2016 via VERY JAZZED (A record label you dink), a subsidiary of Frenchkiss Label Group.

Get real; get hip.
