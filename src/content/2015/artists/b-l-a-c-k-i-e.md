The “one-man noise ordinance” has become legendary in Houston for numerous reasons. Once rumored to perform with over twenty speakers totaling around 5,000 watts. This combination has made his shows become experiences that should be witnessed by everyone. Blackie is known to play secret shows. In fact, he once played on the Hazard Bridge. The musician with all caps and spaces has kept busy since his performance at FPSF several years back. He will play a show at Fitzgerald’s for Halloween. A recent Facebook post by the musician talked about a new release. He will be at the Houston Whatever Fest along with shows in other states including Philadelphia. The crowd at Day For Night will soon learn why B L A C K I E has received the talk he has for his live shows.
