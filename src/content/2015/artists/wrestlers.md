Wrestlers is a local dance band formed in 2012. The group consists of members Aidan Kennedy and David Elkin. The beginning of the band comes from Aidan Kennedy when he was 21. He released a house full length record called “100% Panther.” The band describes their sound Groovy tunes, stellar dance moves, and not too hard on the eyes either.

Kennedy and Elkin’s have since opened for groups such as M83, Passion Pit and Ghostland Observatory. The band’s latest single is entitled “Stanger (Ft.Jesse Boykins III)” on the 8th of October. Wrestlers played the Untapped Festival back in September. They have played many shows around town and have a substantial following.
