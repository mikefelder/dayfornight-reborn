Austin, Texas-born and flower-haired, world-traveled and home-grown, DJ/Artist Christine Renee swims in grey matter connecting musical cell bodies we call house, punk, yacht-rock, rock-rock, disco and nu-beat.

“Christine Renee is living life in a musical dream sending a dance-driven rainbow through the amps for delicious sets dabbling in more genres than joy-filled listeners can think of.” – Honolulu Weekly.

Her career began in NYC mentoring late night discos circa 2001. Working in a Lower Eastside record store she was at an axis of Downtown NY weirdos.

Christine Renee: a “Do” and a “Don’t” – Vice Magazine

A DJ and a Dancer living under Cabaret Law lock-down, CR’s venues began as dive bars and after-hours lofts haunted by mutinous artists who only cared about living even if that meant dying.

Now she plays in Paris, Miami, LA, San Francisco, Mexico City and in Honolulu at DJ Harvey’s invitation.

We’re thrilled to welcome her home for her first Texan performance. It’s about time!

Her performance and community based artwork by has been shown at the Guggenhiem: STAMINA  
Deitch Projects: ART PARADE and DISCO CHICKEN  
MoMa PS1 Warm-up: DJ AFFAIR PROJECT
