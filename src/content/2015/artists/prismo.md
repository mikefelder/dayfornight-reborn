Houston resident Zach Burgett has done many things that DJ’s hope to achieve, and he’s not even 21 yet. As a FPSF alum Prismo knows how the system goes and has executed the right amount of crowd enthusiasm along with a genuine authenticity to him and his music. His latest show in town was at Foam Wonderland, a successful touring EDM festival. Burgett is currently on a tour and recently performed in Colorado.
