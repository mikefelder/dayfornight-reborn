MILLENNIAL GRAVE (aka M. GRAVE) is an electronic music project from Texas native Eric “ceeplus bad knives” Castillo. Primarily a solo performer, M. GRAVE  uses synthesizers, sequencers, drum machines and effects to create moody sonic atmospheres. His music derives from many influences stemming from the sounds of industrial dance, cold synth, kraut rock, early electronics, experimental house / techno / disco, avant garde, soundtrack scores and psychedelic landscapes. His compositions develop and evolve in real time while remaining authentic, intense and cohesive with deliberate and accomplished improvisation while exclusively using electronic instruments. Sorry, no laptops here.  He has performed on bills with GOBLIN, The Soft Moon, JUSTICE, Yppah, Thrill Kill Cult, Psychic TV, Prefuse 73 and more..
