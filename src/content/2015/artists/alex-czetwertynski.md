Alex Czetwertynski is a digital artist working in the fields of creative technology, interactive and experiential design. Born in Belgium, he graduated with a Masters in Philosophy from the Sorbonne University in Paris. After founding and steering the creative/production agency Disciple Films, he moved to Los Angeles and shifted towards experiential design, working as a creative lead on the worldwide tours of artists such as Stevie Wonder, The Strokes, Rihanna, Beyonce and Madonna. Since then his practice has evolved towards large scale immersive experiences, for which he designs interaction, motion and physical presence.He has collaborated with artists such as Doug Aitken, Jessica Mitrani and ThreeAsFour. His work has been shown at the Jewish Museum and the FIAC in New York, The Centre George Pompidou and the Theatre de la Cite in Paris, The Mint Museum in Charlotte NC, The Columbus College of Art and Design in Columbus OH, and the Dubai Design Week among others.
