Flcon Fcker, the mysterious local musician, has made a name for himself, or herself? Maybe even itself? Perhaps that’s the point, the thirty-something year old electronic musician has separated critical fame around the local music scene from his true identity, like Deadmau5 or Daft Punk, I suppose? Past this mystery lies great talent not only with the definite music but alongside the little information we know about Flcon Fcker. He has a degree in Biology and Chemistry and nowadays enjoys making synthesizers. His shows on the other hand are no mystery. He consistently holds steady crowd sizes and maintains a reliable fanbase.

Flcon Fcker has played at many venues across town including Fitzgerald’s and Numbers, just to name a couple.
