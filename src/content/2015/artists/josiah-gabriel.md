Josiah Gabriel is a local producer and DJ. He started making music under as a solo musician in 2010. He has released one album, entitled “Notion of the Life.” Gabriel has also released individual songs since the release. His latest work was a feature on a song by musician Standard. Josiah Gabriel’s latest show was at Jet Lounge. Other venues he’s played includes Numbers and the Boulevardier Lounge. He has been nominated for the Houston Press Music Awards for Best Producer.
