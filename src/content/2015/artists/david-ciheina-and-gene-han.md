David Cihelna builds and directs entertainment experiments at NYU’s Interactive Telecommunications Program. His work includes InConcert (a multi-device concert film), Glitch The World (a mobile experience that lets you chat with fictional characters), and Virtual Reality filmmaking research. Originally a music producer from Vienna, Austria he currently resides in New York City. Gene Han is a former game developer who is currently a Master’s Candidate at NYU’s Interactive Telecommunications Program. He focuses on Interactive installations, augmented/virtual reality and artificial intelligence. His previous projects includes Limitless Dash (machine learning connected car) and Sentient Surveillance Camera (speaking AI surveillance).
