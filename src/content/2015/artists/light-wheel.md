The recently formed group Light Wheel was formed in Austin, Tx. The group is comprised of Producer Evan Dunivan and Vocalist Tyagaraja. Self-described as aesthetic pop with touches of R&B grooves and Electronic flair, Light Wheel has released three songs under the outfit. With their music marked by colorful soundscapes and eclectic rhythms, it is a unique spin on the music Tyagaraja and Evan Dunivan enjoy.

The group is ecstatic about their passion of music, as well as to perform at Day for Night. However, this is certainly not the first tour for Tygaraja. The musician/yoga instructor has previously toured India, planning to visit the country again soon after the festival. The first single, The Keeper, is a blend of sweeping vocals, huge dynamics and intuitive rhythms.
