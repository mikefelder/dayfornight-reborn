Experimental electronic music producer [Flying Lotus](http://www.allmusic.com/artist/flying-lotus-mn0000717419), born [Steven Ellison](http://www.allmusic.com/artist/steven-ellison-mn0002091303), is a grandson of songwriter [Marilyn McLeod](http://www.allmusic.com/artist/marilyn-mcleod-mn0000826205)(the co-writer of [Diana Ross](http://www.allmusic.com/artist/diana-ross-mn0000594665)‘ “Love Hangover”), as well as a great-nephew of pianist [Alice Coltrane](http://www.allmusic.com/artist/alice-coltrane-mn0000006143), and therefore a cousin of saxophonist [Ravi Coltrane](http://www.allmusic.com/artist/ravi-coltrane-mn0000401568). [Ellison](http://www.allmusic.com/artist/ellison-mn0002091303) made beats for the Cartoon Network’s Adult Swim network before releasing his debut full-length, [1983](http://www.allmusic.com/album/1983-mw0000448376), which resembled the work of fellow avant-garde hip-hop producers [Madlib](http://www.allmusic.com/artist/madlib-mn0000238375), [J Dilla](http://www.allmusic.com/artist/j-dilla-mn0000428126), and [Ammoncontact](http://www.allmusic.com/artist/ammoncontact-mn0000748070) on Plug Research in 2006. Following the six-song EP [Reset](http://www.allmusic.com/album/reset-mw0000493542), he released his second full-length, [Los Angeles](http://www.allmusic.com/album/los-angeles-mw0000788516), on Warp in 2008. A three-part series of satellite EPs consisting of remixes and additional productions trailed through the next year. [Cosmogramma](http://www.allmusic.com/album/cosmogramma-mw0001965699), his third and most complex album, was issued on the same label in 2010, while the relatively pared-down [Until the Quiet Comes](http://www.allmusic.com/album/until-the-quiet-comes-mw0002410175) followed in 2012. The suite-like [You’re Dead!](http://www.allmusic.com/album/youre-dead%21-mw0002720542), on which he was joined by [Herbie Hancock](http://www.allmusic.com/artist/herbie-hancock-mn0000957296), [Kendrick Lamar](http://www.allmusic.com/artist/kendrick-lamar-mn0002709646), and [Snoop Dogg](http://www.allmusic.com/artist/snoop-dogg-mn0000029086), and [Captain Murphy](http://www.allmusic.com/artist/captain-murphy-mn0003320030) ([Ellison](http://www.allmusic.com/artist/ellison-mn0002091303)‘s rapping alias), among others, came two years later. [Ellison](http://www.allmusic.com/artist/ellison-mn0002091303)‘s considerable quantity of additional production and remix work is scattered across dozens of releases on revered labels such as [Tectonic](http://www.allmusic.com/artist/tectonic-mn0001831758), Hyperdub, Ghostly International, and Ninja Tune — the last of which is the distributor of his Brainfeeder label, home to releases by the likes of [the Gaslamp Killer](http://www.allmusic.com/artist/the-gaslamp-killer-mn0000573228), [Austin Peralta](http://www.allmusic.com/artist/austin-peralta-mn0001419089), [Martyn](http://www.allmusic.com/artist/martyn-mn0002026475), frequent collaborator [Thundercat](http://www.allmusic.com/artist/thundercat-mn0002424073), and [Taylor McFerrin](http://www.allmusic.com/artist/taylor-mcferrin-mn0001881073).
