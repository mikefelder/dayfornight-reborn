Mark Eats is an audio-visual artist and musician experimenting with code, animation, performance and sound. He has created playful installations, films, performances and interactive projects. From reflections of city life to interventions in suburbia. Originally from England, Mark Eats is now based in Los Angeles where he is finding new ways to compose music and imagery as singular pieces.
